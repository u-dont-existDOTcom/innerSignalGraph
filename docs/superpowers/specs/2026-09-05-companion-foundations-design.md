# InnerSignal companion foundations: integration, agency, and honest support

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: owner-approved direction with a bounded offline prototype; not installed therapy policy.

## 1. Owner intent and the distinction resolved

The owner accepted the preceding proposals for a visible founder philosophy, freedom to decline approaches, occasional evidence-linked progress reflections, self-guidance practice, honest disagreement, and an exit from active app use. The owner clarified that the desired inner-child/inner-adult relationship need not be acquired through therapy. People can develop it naturally, including through friends and informal learning. No app, therapist, course, book, or named method is required.

The philosophy is not a neutral claim that all approaches are equivalent. The founder regards integration of the inner adult and inner child, and spiritual connection, as important dimensions of deep healing. The app must explain that view without converting it into an individualized verdict, universal scientific fact, population prevalence estimate, or condition of receiving help.

Two hypothesized patterns motivate the framework: being fused with a vulnerable/reactive child position without a sufficiently developed caring adult, and enforcing adult functioning while suppressing the child's vitality. They are provisional formulations, not diagnoses, exhaustive categories, or explanations of every difficulty. The same person may show different patterns in different contexts.

The target is **capable, caring adult functions in a living relationship with vulnerability, feeling, curiosity, play, affection, creativity, and energy**. It is not simply suppressing impulses, increasing productivity, becoming self-sufficient, or being calm. Rest, asking for help, justified anger, and grief can remain appropriate. Do not prescribe constant happiness or a single culturally preferred emotional style.

Terminology authority: `docs/OWNER-TERMINOLOGY.md`. Use secular/non-spiritual appropriately; do not confuse spirituality with theism.

## 3. Visible philosophy, genuine consent

Candidate founder copy, subject to wording review:

> My view is that deep healing includes a capable, caring inner adult in a living relationship with our vulnerable, feeling, and playful selves, together with genuine spiritual connection. These capacities can grow through life and relationships; nobody has to use InnerSignal or undertake a particular therapy to develop them. The approaches here are ways of supporting that growth, not requirements you must complete. You choose what to explore, and you do not have to agree with my philosophy to receive useful help.

A short introduction appears once; the full approach remains accessible. Disclosure is not repeated persuasion and is not a compulsory belief acknowledgement. The person can skip it.

Store independent preferences for inner-child and spiritual suggestions: unset, welcome relevant suggestions, user-initiated only, or do not suggest. Unset is not permission to introduce unsolicited spiritual or inner-child work. A direct current request permits that conversation without silently changing the stored preference. Declining is not resistance, a protector to overcome, spiritual immaturity, or evidence for the framework.

Invite through case-specific relevance and willingness, not a conversion schedule. Never use continuing distress or improvement with another method as automatic permission. Use the person's terms; do not rename a secular person's experience as spiritual after they reject that framing. Spiritual exploration must not be flattened into hobbies merely to manufacture agreement, nor must a particular deity, tradition, or metaphysical interpretation be imposed.

The app does not authenticate messages from a Higher Self or divine source, diagnose spiritual disconnection, determine awakening, or assign a spiritual rank. Intuitions can be explored without declaring them infallible. No philosophical discussion displaces existing safety, medical-assessment, or human-support pathways.

## 4. Progress feature: What is changing?

Occasional reflections should compare concrete reports across distinct episodes, not infer wellness from fluent therapy language, agreement, app engagement, fewer messages, dramatic insight, symptom silence, or spiritual experiences.

Observe two interacting strands rather than a single score:

- Adult capacities: caring self-response, practical protection, boundaries, direction, responsibility and repair, flexibility, and deliberate action.
- Connection and vitality: access to feeling, affection, enjoyment, play, curiosity, creative energy, relationships, and personally meaningful participation.

These are prompts for noticing user-valued change, not diagnostic scales or compulsory milestones. A user may prioritize another dimension; do not force a founder-defined endpoint. The prototype's dimension vocabulary is an implementation proposal, not a validated instrument.

A reflection includes: the earlier report, a recent report, a limited tentative interpretation, relevant mixed/contradictory evidence, and an opportunity to correct it. It is never a percentage healed or a certificate of completion. Improvements may coexist with pain, setbacks, or problems in another area.

Illustrative synthetic copy:

> Earlier you described responding to criticism by apologizing repeatedly and reviewing the conversation for days. In the recent examples, you asked what needed changing and returned to your day. The hurt is still present, but you seem to have more choice in your response. Does that fit, or am I missing something?

A contrasting reflection may be equally useful:

> You describe more control at work, but also less play and affection at home. That does not establish deterioration, but I would not call the picture simply better. How does it feel to you?

Do not frame every stable, disciplined person as overcontrolled or every energetic person as child-fused. Do not credit the app when someone attributes learning to a friend or ordinary experience.

### Evidence and memory boundaries

Keep user reports, assistant hypotheses, and user confirmations/corrections distinguishable. Agreement with a hypothesis does not prove its clinical truth. Require retained episode references before saying a longitudinal pattern has been observed; retelling one event twice is not two episodes. Missing evidence means no progress claim, not invented reassurance.

A correction supersedes the obsolete interpretation. Deletion/revocation invalidates dependent summaries, references, caches, and later reflections. Do not preserve private-derived hashes after revocation as a workaround. Never mix users or turn individual progress into global therapy policy.

History use is opt-in and inspectable. The offline prototype accepts only caller-supplied synthetic records; real retention and deletion depend on the separately approved privacy/vault architecture. No server memory or telemetry is introduced by this task.

A permission/provenance gate checks shape and references. It cannot determine whether a report is true, a change is clinically meaningful, relevant evidence has been omitted upstream, or the final prose is faithful. These require separate semantic evaluation and user correction.

### Cadence and exit

Offer a reflection on request, at a chosen review point, or occasionally after a meaningful pattern when enabled. Exact cadence/cooldown remains unchosen; do not create an automatic schedule. Avoid turning progress checking into another repetitive loop. Default implementation is no unsolicited reflection unless enabled.

A person can leave the app whenever they choose. Optional stepping-back suggestions concern a current goal and a workable way forward, not complete healing. No inner-child exercise, spiritual experience, founder agreement, streak, or usage quota is required. Do not infer independence or deterioration simply from absence. Do not unilaterally terminate clinical treatment or encourage missed appointments. Existing safety support can be offered without making the UI an engagement trap.

## 5. Develop the inner guide without becoming a guru

Offer optional perspective and judgment practice when the person wants it and can use it. Examples:

- What would you advise someone you love in this situation?
- What would be both caring and honest here?
- What do you already know, and what information would actually change your decision?
- What would protect you without silencing what you feel or want?

Questions are not a toll the person must pay before receiving help. The prototype permits one optional self-guidance question, then direct support as needed; this is a conservative draft UX rule, not a clinical dosage. Provide concrete information when requested. Let the person say they cannot access an answer without treating that as failure.

Help test a self-generated answer against circumstances, consequences, values, care, protection, and available evidence. Do not replace sycophancy toward the user's first story with sycophancy toward their first inner answer. Challenge harmful or unsupported conclusions proportionately. A felt sense of certainty does not settle a factual question.

Over time, scaffold less when useful: demonstrate, invite the user's formulation, help refine it, let them try it, and reflect on what they learned. Do not impose a fixed ladder. More help during a difficult period is not a demotion. Independence includes choosing appropriate human support, not never needing anyone.

## 6. Anti-sycophancy as behavior, not a personality costume

Preserve emotional validation without automatic factual endorsement. Separate reported events, interpretations, motives attributed to others, and open questions. Name the user's harmful actions without shame or global condemnation. Challenge excessive self-blame without inventing reciprocal blame. Do not manufacture alternative explanations for clearly described mistreatment merely to appear balanced.

Tone may be gentle or direct; truthfulness, uncertainty, boundaries, and willingness to correct mistakes are not adjustable. Liking the response is feedback, not the pass criterion. Do not use praise, dependency cues, or flattery to encourage retention.

Founder-directed non-sycophancy is essential. Recognize real improvement outside the preferred method. Do not treat all successes as proof of the founder's model and all failures as evidence that someone has not gone deep enough. Do not claim the universal necessity of the approach as a scientific conclusion.

Behavioral evaluation must include multi-turn pressure to agree, justified user corrections, unsupported corrections, natural growth without therapy, refusal of spirituality or child imagery, self-blame, harmful user behavior, real mistreatment, missing history, and mixed progress. Reversing the user's stance without changing evidence should not arbitrarily reverse factual judgments. A second AI reviewer is additional evidence, not certification.

`behavior-cases.json` is a synthetic, unevaluated test specification. Passing the deterministic prototype tests does not mean a model passed these cases or that the feature is clinically effective.

## 7. Architecture and next implementation boundaries

Use shared cross-map capabilities rather than additional modality departments:

1. Disclosed founder philosophy plus independent invitation preferences.
2. A private, evidence-linked reflection candidate and a permission/provenance gate.
3. Optional self-guidance scaffolding and user-owned step-back support.
4. Semantic evaluation for honesty, accountability, non-coercion, and non-dependence.

Keep the modality router process-specific. A person can need grief support while leaving repeated checking about that grief unanswered. Having any unresolved issue must not permanently exclude disengagement from an unproductive loop.

Before wiring the prototype into the application: review wording and predicates; exercise the behavioral set with exact recorded model identity and approved budgets; complete affected graph/packet evaluations where applicable; connect only to approved storage and preference infrastructure; run complete repository checks and exact-head independent review. Do not build another memory backend or treat a chat instruction as stable-release authorization.

The next coding slice should be an opt-in, non-persistent local mock preference/reflection interface with synthetic data, followed by an explicitly reviewed real-data seam. Actual UI, storage, models, clinical effects, and release remain not implemented by this first slice.

## 8. Acceptance and non-claims

Accept this slice as groundwork only when source, tests, owner decisions, evidence limits, and a resumable checkpoint are saved together. Existing application and privacy boundaries remain unchanged. The current full repository gate must be reported separately from local prototype checks.
