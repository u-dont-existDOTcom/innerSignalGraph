# A001 outcome-first design

## Objective

Optimize Inner Signal’s response and immediate follow-up process for the exact original A001 question until the owner finds an actual generated answer genuinely useful enough to continue with. This is a focused outcome demonstration, not a broad therapy-protocol architecture project.

## Authority and boundaries

- Start from protected `origin/main` at `a22f2e611fab778bf26b8e7215afbf85aba4ba5e`.
- Preserve PR #11 as parked research; import no wholesale code or policy from it.
- Keep `stable` unchanged.
- Do not resume broad corpora, general trajectories, Map 15/16, taxonomy, ontology, release, or unrelated work.
- Never store private therapy transcripts. The owner-supplied A001 text is explicitly authorized as the task fixture; model artifacts must remain sanitized and credential-free.
- Do not diagnose the owner or claim treatment efficacy, healing, integration, trust, or therapy outcome from a strong response.

## Work order

1. Preserve the independent conception.
2. Perform a bounded scan of established work and choose reuse/adapt/compose explicitly.
3. Capture static and actual live production baselines with exact identities and sanitized outputs.
4. Develop only four materially different arms: production baseline, credibility-repair experiment, differentiation/chairwork, and integrated minimal route.
5. Test the ten owner-specified A001 follow-up trajectories.
6. Evaluate candidates through physically separated, blinded Opus and Codex inputs; use Fable only for verified unresolved adjudication.
7. Present the best two or three answers in randomized anonymized order and stop for owner experiential judgment.
8. Implement only the winning elements, then run focused and required repository verification and prepare a focused draft PR.

## Intervention design constraints

The response must distinguish accessible love from unsafe affiliation, regulation from relational repair, and vows from observable evidence. It must clarify the blamed age and actual agency, state that a literal child was not responsible for raising itself, preserve constrained but nonzero agency for adolescents or younger adults, address resentment without letting contempt parent, collaboratively define one small observable time-bounded test, permit skepticism, define missed-promise repair without care arrears, check present external reality, and end with one precise next interaction.

Naturalness matters. The first response is not required to dump the entire later process. A candidate hard-fails if it leads with generic regulation absent present need, sentimentalizes an inner-child voice, assigns roles as facts, demands trust or gratitude, makes a grand vow, ignores resentment or current life, treats one action as proof, creates punitive arrears, or adds irrelevant crisis/referral boilerplate.

- Separate static mock, live production, grader expectations, and independent evaluations.

## Evaluation firewall

Producer inputs and evaluator inputs must be separate files or generated artifacts. The evaluator sees only the exact original message, one candidate response, and the task-specific rubric/hard failures. It does not see arm/model identity, preferred wording, implementation details, producer rationale, or another evaluator’s result. First-pass findings freeze before reconciliation.

The rubric is an engineering filter, not a validated clinical measure: exact fit, felt understanding, credibility rupture, age/agency, resentment, non-retaliation, actionability, missed-promise repair, external reality, conversational burden, and desire to continue. Any hard failure disqualifies a candidate regardless of average score.

## Owner gate

Only after independent filtering, randomize and anonymize the best two or three answers. Ask the owner about understanding, falseness/irritation/manipulation/genericness, useful next move, willingness to continue, any change in understanding or intended behavior, and 0–10 usefulness. Do not reveal provenance until after rating. No policy encoding occurs before this gate.

## Recovery architecture

`tasks/ACTIVE-TASK.json`, `tasks/START-HERE.md`, and `state/CODEX-CURRENT-STATE.md` are the recovery entrypoints. Each meaningful checkpoint records branch/SHA, evidence, uncertainty, and next action in GitHub. Conversation is disposable working memory; Git and exact artifacts are durable authority.
