# InnerSignal continuity, scaffolded challenge, and community support

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: owner-authorized development change

## Owner outcome

Repair the routing failure exposed in the Pamela test without encoding Pamela or any private case facts. InnerSignal must:
- preserve inner-child reparenting as a continuing relationship rather than abandoning the modality when depth needs to change;
- use the gentlest viable form before a full pause, and give any full pause explicit reactivation conditions;
- distinguish difficult but workable therapeutic experience from actual overload/harm, titrating challenge through scaffolding/pendulation instead of treating distress or a bad dream as failure;
- let ordinary actions and outside support count as Nurturer/Protector/Guide behavior when they genuinely serve those functions;
- help socially isolated people diversify real human connection rather than making one person, invitation, group, therapist, or spiritual authority the single point of support;
- keep unusual or medically suggestive side details from hijacking the client's expressed primary therapeutic focus unless the answer would materially change immediate safety or the next step;
- for non-urgent possible medical side issues, establish whether the experience is new/different from baseline and whether it has already been assessed before implying that prior care is absent.

## Evidence boundary

The owner-framework claim is not that distress is therapeutic by itself. Difficult dreams, grief, fear, tears, or activation may be workable and informative, but whether a challenge is useful is judged by the person's experience, orientation, ability to stop/return, later functioning/recovery, and whether it yields usable information/capacity rather than escalating disorganization. Do not assert that dreams reveal hidden truth or that adversity necessarily causes growth.
