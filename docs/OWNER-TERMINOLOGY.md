# Owner terminology and framing

Status: owner-directed project convention, recorded 2026-09-05.
Applies to founder copy, product design, examples, prompts, tests, and future handoffs.
This is a durable project record, not confirmation of an account-wide ChatGPT memory update.

## Secular and non-spiritual are not synonyms for nontheistic

Use **secular** or **non-spiritual**, according to the person's own description, when contrasting a secular approach with a spiritual approach. Do not use **atheist** as shorthand for non-spiritual. A position concerning gods does not establish whether a person has a spiritual practice or connection; in particular, do not erase nontheistic Buddhist spirituality. Do not infer an individual's worldview from a religious affiliation or assign labels they reject.

The owner explicitly reaffirmed this correction. Future workers must preserve it instead of asking the owner to repeat it.

## Integration, not compulsory participation

Within the founder's philosophy, a developed inner adult in a living relationship with the inner child, and genuine spiritual connection, are necessary dimensions of complete healing, not merely optional add-ons. The necessity concerns the developed relationship/capacities and connection, not participation in a named therapy. This is the founder's philosophy, not an established universal clinical necessity or a population statistic.

Those capacities can develop through ordinary life, friendships, example, relationships, and informal learning. Therapy, InnerSignal, books, explicit imagery, a named modality, and adopting this vocabulary are not prerequisites. Credit growth outside the app and do not relabel it as app-caused.

The aim is neither child-fused reactivity nor a controlling adult that suppresses vulnerability, enjoyment, spontaneity, and energy. Do not infer either pattern from diagnosis, symptoms, occupational success, disagreement, or limited information alone. Do not determine a user's spiritual attainment or use an attainment label as a product exception.
