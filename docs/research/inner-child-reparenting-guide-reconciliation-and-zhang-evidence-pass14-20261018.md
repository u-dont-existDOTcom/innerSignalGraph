### Claims verified against Zhang's *cited underlying research*, not the unavailable PDF

- **PTSD nonresponse ~39%:** supported by Semmlinger et al. (2024), *Prevalence and Predictors of Nonresponse to Psychological Treatment for PTSD: A Meta-Analysis*, `https://doi.org/10.1155/2024/9899034`. **86 RCTs, 117 active treatments, 7,894 participants; pooled 39.23% [95% CI 35.08–43.53]**. Importantly this is not a universal constant or proof of any ontology: rate varied **0–85.71%** across studies, between-study heterogeneity **I² ~83%**. The paper grouped first-line treatments for adult diagnosed PTSD and different nonresponse definitions, often retention of diagnosis. **Conclusion:** Zhang's approximate 39% datum is genuine, but inferring her consciousness-field model as the missing cause is **unsupported**.
- **Body-oriented trauma treatment durability:** van de Kamp et al. (2023), `https://doi.org/10.1002/jts.22968`, meta-analysis of **29 studies** with mean **Hedges g ≈ 0.50** and **very high heterogeneity, I² ~89%**, cautioned that most included studies had some concern/high risk of bias; only **one** low risk. At **6–12 months only two studies** contributed to the specific follow-up estimate; none justified the inference that the FHEES stages fix durability. Supports a research gap, not Zhang's proposed mechanism.
- **“Dodo bird” / approximate equivalence:** Wampold's classic perspective is a view in psychotherapy research, **not consensus that all genuine therapies have indistinguishable effects on every primary outcome**. A later comparative meta-analysis, `https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0272735814001184`, found **small differences for primary outcomes at treatment termination**, while secondary outcomes tended to be more similar. Therefore the website's “very different therapies produce roughly equivalent outcomes” needs qualified, context-specific phrasing; it cannot itself single out a common metaphysical baseline.
- **FHEES components:** Feel/Heal/Entangle/Endure/Synchronize combine familiar emotion regulation, attachment work, social network and habit-maintenance efforts with original speculations about fields and synchronicities. Evidence for some individual components **does not validate** the combined protocol, its five-stage ordering, claimed permanence, “healing backlash” illness, or cosmic pattern interpretations. The author's stated status correctly separates these. The dated and humanized guide already teaches social safety, community, inward care, ordinary protective behavior, and verifying intuitions against real outcomes.
