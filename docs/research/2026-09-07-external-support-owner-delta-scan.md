# External support owner delta: bounded source scan

Research date: 2026-09-07. Candidate architecture evidence only; no provider discovery, clinical assessment, runtime activation or release approval.

## Conception and scope

The independent requirements and predictions were frozen before this scan in [the integration ledger](../../tasks/external-support-20260907/INTEGRATION.md). This scan checks those requirements against ten decisive first-party or official sources. It does not independently validate the proposed interview, scoring, trial schedule or assembled support package. No private case data was searched or reproduced.

Search formulations included `IPS principles zero exclusion client preferences competitive employment`; `care farming code practice green care therapeutic structured programme`; `WWOOF educational exchange`; `NICE NG181 potential supported accommodation visit employment`; `NICE NG150 willingness carers breaks`; `SAMHSA peer worker core functions boundaries`; `FTC coaching programs high pressure sales financing`; `FTC health products compliance evidence testimonials`; and `NCCIH anxiety relaxation trauma safety meditation adverse effects`. Searches used public web search, followed by source-page opening and focused passage searches. Search-result discovery alone was not treated as proof of efficacy.

## Source register and decisions

1. **[IPS Employment Center: IPS Practice and Principles](https://ipsworks.org/index.php/documents/ips-practice-and-principles/)**, handout revision June 2024. **Type/access:** first-party model specification; relevant HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** eight principles, especially eligibility, preferences, integration, benefits advice, rapid search and continuing individualized support. IPS does not exclude people because of symptoms or a provider's readiness judgment. **Decision: reuse the service baseline; adapt the interface.** Ask whether the person wants employment and what support it needs. Assess whether a particular placement can meet needs without turning that assessment into a general employment-eligibility barrier. This source specifies IPS; it does not establish that volunteering, farm exchange or the candidate architecture produces IPS outcomes.

2. **[NICE NG181: Rehabilitation for adults with complex psychosis](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG181/chapter/recommendations)**, recommendations 1.3.7, 1.3.11–12, 1.5.9 and 1.8.9–14. **Type/access:** clinical guideline; relevant indexed HTML/PDF passages verified, but direct HTML/PDF opening returned 403. Underlying evidence reviews were not assessed. **Verified passage:** flexible support according to changing needs; visits before supported-accommodation moves; coordinated transitions; support fitted to health needs; education, IPS and other occupational opportunities. **Decision: adapt and compose.** Use visits and handover as a baseline for informed environmental-fit decisions. Educational or voluntary alternatives remain choices, not compulsory stages before IPS. This guideline concerns complex psychosis, explicitly excluding primary non-psychotic conditions from its main scope; broader transfer is a design inference. It does not validate the project's visit/trial durations or general farm-placement sequence.

3. **[Social Farms & Gardens: Growing Care Farming](https://www.farmgarden.org.uk/growing-care-farming)**. **Type/access:** first-party sector description; HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** “What care farms do” and “Why care farming works” describe planned, supervised activity tailored to people with identified needs, including purposeful contribution. **Decision: reuse the category distinction.** Care farming requires a support programme; a rural setting alone does not establish it. Verify a particular provider's staffing, programme, competence and referral arrangements separately. The organization's promotional benefit statements are not comparative trial evidence, an assurance about every listed provider, or proof of effectiveness for a particular trauma presentation.

4. **[WWOOF: What is WWOOF?](https://wwoof.net/)**. **Type/access:** first-party programme description; relevant HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** educational and cultural exchange, participation in host life and farm activity, with food and accommodation. **Decision: reuse the ordinary-exchange baseline.** Treat an ordinary exchange as a possible chosen environment/activity, with its own fit and verification questions. Nothing in the reviewed description establishes clinical staffing, crisis response, supported accommodation or therapeutic equivalence to care farming. Absence of money exchanged with a host is not evidence of zero travel, membership, insurance or exit costs. No individual host was assessed.

5. **[FTC: When a Business Offer or Coaching Program Is a Scam](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/when-business-offer-or-coaching-program-scam)**, August 2022. **Type/access:** official consumer-protection guidance; HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** avoidance and pre-purchase sections address urgency, escalating purchases, misleading testimonials, credential checks, independent discussion and affordability. **Decision: adapt consumer safeguards.** Record total commitment, pressure, recurring charges and exit/refund terms before an offer can pass financial fit. Refuse to treat urgency or an expensive package as evidence of therapeutic necessity. Applying these checks to wellness coaching is a cautious transfer from business-coaching guidance, not a finding that every coach is fraudulent or that a particular contract breaches local law.

6. **[FTC: Health Products Compliance Guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/health-products-compliance-guidance)**, 2022. **Type/access:** official staff advertising guidance; HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** preface and sections II–III distinguish express/implied claims, scientific substantiation and disclosure of material limits and risks. **Decision: reuse claim discipline; adapt screening.** A provider's advertised benefit, a person's reported benefit, and evidence for the particular method/population are separate records. An affordable method's possible usefulness and an expensive provider's sales conduct must be judged separately. This is US guidance about health-related product advertising, explicitly without independent force of law; it supplies neither a worldwide legal ruling nor a clinical verdict about a wellness service.

7. **[SAMHSA TIP 64, chapter 3: Peer Worker Core Functions](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK596270/)**, 2023. **Type/access:** official practice guidance hosted by NCBI; relevant indexed chapter passages verified; direct chapter access failed and printable access returned 403. **Verified passage:** “Peer Specialist Boundaries,” “Respecting Professional Boundaries,” supervision and Exhibit 3.2 distinguish recovery support from clinical roles and recommend help before becoming overwhelmed. **Decision: adapt relationship safeguards.** Check role clarity, voluntary participation, capacity, reciprocity expectations and access to wider support. Formal peer-worker guidance does not automatically govern friendship. It supports reviewing overload and boundaries, not categorically ending relationships with people who struggle. No validated rescue-burden score or relationship-exit rule was found in this bounded scan.

8. **[NICE NG150: Supporting adult carers](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng150/chapter/recommendations)**, recommendations 1.3.4, 1.3.10–12, 1.5.2–4 and 1.7.1–5. **Type/access:** official guideline; relevant indexed passages verified; direct HTML access returned 403. Underlying reviews were not assessed. **Verified passage:** assess carers' own priorities; do not presume ability or willingness; discuss shared responsibilities, breaks and support for carers. **Decision: adapt a separate supporter-capacity check.** A close relationship does not silently supply unlimited care. Where support is wanted but insufficient, seek additional help and retain the uncovered need. The source concerns adult caring relationships and a particular social-care system; it does not establish universal service entitlements or make reciprocity a condition of deserving support.

9. **[NCCIH: Anxiety and Complementary Health Approaches](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/anxiety-and-complementary-health-approaches)**, page marked September 2024. **Type/access:** official research synthesis and safety information; HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** the relaxation safety subsection describes occasional increased anxiety, intrusive thoughts or fear of losing control, and rare reports of worsening in some trauma-related or other conditions. **Decision: adapt method-specific harm questions.** Record what was used, context, intensity, immediate and later experiences, functional effects and available qualified support. Those candidate monitoring fields are a precautionary design choice. Findings about relaxation practices do not establish efficacy, mechanisms, incidence of harm, contraindications or a dosing schedule for shaking/TRE or every somatic method.

10. **[NCCIH: Meditation and Mindfulness—Effectiveness and Safety](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/meditation-and-mindfulness-effectiveness-and-safety)**, page marked June 2022. **Type/access:** official research synthesis; HTML read directly. **Verified passage:** safety section notes limited investigation of harms and both negative-experience findings and a narrower analysis finding no greater harm than no treatment; practical advice addresses instructor experience and coordination with health care. **Decision: reuse uncertainty and monitoring discipline.** Do not translate sparse harm reporting into established safety, or an unpleasant experience automatically into lasting harm. This scan does not transfer a meditation adverse-effect percentage or effectiveness result to a different somatic practice, general trauma care or an individual person.

## External baselines and candidate composition

The usable external baselines are: choice-led supported employment with continuing support; purpose and occupation within rehabilitation; supported rather than merely rural care; visits before accommodation decisions; consumer checks independent of claimed benefit; defined peer roles and supporter limits; and explicit attention to method-specific harms and uncertainty. These are existing approaches to reuse or adapt, not discoveries of this project. The combined routing architecture remains an unvalidated composition.

The following remain **owner/project defaults**, not conclusions supplied by these sources: making meaningful contribution a core support function when wanted and feasible; the interview order and prior-treatment-failure model; particular reversible trial lengths; all numerical fit weights or thresholds; hard-constraint preservation when resources are scarce; and the rule that an unsuccessful environmental trial revises fit without blaming the person. A preferred activity must not become forced productivity, and a trial must not replace required support. A titration/review option for a helpful method is a candidate process safeguard, not a prescription or proof that supervision eliminates its risks.

For scarce resources, compose only functions that can actually be covered and retain each gap explicitly. A modular package is not evidence of equivalence to an integrated service. Unknown critical staffing, crisis, affordability or exit facts remain unknown; proximity, an appealing website or a friendly host cannot fill them.

## Uncertain remainder and completion boundary

This bounded scan did not estimate comparative effects, local availability, wait times, individual eligibility, actual costs, provider competence, or the safety and effectiveness of shaking/TRE. It did not inspect private provider records, contact services, or assess a real person. It cannot settle whether a particular distress response is tolerable transient discomfort, destabilization or a reason for clinical escalation. Those decisions require context and appropriate human assessment.

The sources justify concrete questions and distinctions. They do not validate automated clinical clearance, diagnosis-based exclusion, a total-cure promise, fixed treatment doses, or a claim that every desirable support function can currently be supplied. Ten sources were retained; overlapping search hits and inaccessible underlying evidence were not promoted into additional evidence claims.
