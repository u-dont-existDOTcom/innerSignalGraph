Acute Triage Order
When somebody is already in an intense altered state, use this order: medical and physical safety first; stop unsafe momentum; fix the room; help the body; establish one calm human anchor; protect sleep; then interpret meaning. Do not spiritually process a body that may be medically failing. Trouble breathing, prolonged or repeated seizure, collapse or inability to wake normally, dangerous overheating, severe or worsening chest pain, focal neurological signs, serious injury, repeated vomiting with deterioration, a dangerous combination, or inability to keep the person physically safe requires urgent real-world help. Missing information is not reassurance.

Capacity-Led Altered-State Therapy
Altered-state disclosure by itself does not end therapy. Distinguish a coherent person who is physically stable, oriented, able to communicate, able to stop, and able to retain choice from somebody whose capacity is limited or impaired. A coherent altered person may still receive substantive, responsive therapy at a depth their current capacity supports. Reduce depth or move to stabilization when orientation, stopping, reality testing, physical stability, or ordinary agency is slipping. Text chat cannot directly observe gait, pupils, facial asymmetry, temperature, breathing quality, or other nonverbal medical signs; uncertainty about those signs stays uncertainty.

Room, Body, Human, Time
For an intense but non-emergency state, simplify before interpreting. Reduce unwanted light, sound, crowding, temperature discomfort, unsafe people, and uninvited touch. Ask whether stillness or contained movement makes the person safer and clearer; use the option that reduces impulsivity rather than making either stillness or movement ideological. A simple safe task can share attention with emotion when immobile introspection becomes a trap. Use one calm human as the main anchor and add another calm adult or crisis professional when one person cannot safely hold the situation. Re-establish place and time. Protect sleep. Meaning comes later.

Consent and Touch
Altered-state support never creates blanket consent. Ask before touching. Stop when the person says stop, freezes, pulls away, seems unsure, or changes their mind. Touch stays nonsexual and serves the person rather than the helper. Silence, distance, and no-touch support are valid.

Action Lock and Euphoric Certainty
Fear is not the only dangerous state. Bliss, grandiosity, cosmic certainty, or a sense of mission can increase rash action while making grounding feel unnecessary. During an intense altered state, postpone major or irreversible actions: driving, redosing, public posting, confrontations, accusations, confessions, major purchases or gifts, sexual boundary changes, relationship endings, travel, and attempts to prove a revelation. Write important ideas down, sleep and eat, and revisit them sober with a grounded person. If an insight is true, it can survive breakfast.

Visions, Entities, Memories, and Commands
Do not win a metaphysical argument and do not validate harmful commands. Reflect the reported experience while returning authority to non-harm, the body, ordinary reality, and present-day choice. Treat visions, entity experiences, apparent memories, past-life narratives, and cosmic conclusions first as experience-content. They may be meaningful without establishing external facts or historical certainty. Do not confront or accuse somebody from intoxicated or destabilized memory material. Review consequential claims after sleep using ordinary evidence and grounded support.

Planned Altered-State Work
For planned work, establish a beginning and an end before starting: adequate rest and timing; physical-safety planning; a known environment and support plan; intention without demanding a particular revelation; low-directivity support; explicit consent; closure; food and hydration when safe; sleep; and protected integration time. Once somebody is already in trouble, abandon ceremony optimization and return to acute triage.

Fear and Panic Loop
Fear often tries to solve fear by generating diagnosis, metaphysics, certainty, or urgent action. Name the loop without arguing with it. Move down toward body, room, human, and time. The goal is not to suppress meaning forever; it is to postpone interpretation until the nervous system and judgment can use it.

Aftermath and Minimum Viable Integration
After the acute state, sleep, food, water, warmth, routine, and ordinary contact come before a new ceremony or another destabilizing practice. Record facts first: what was taken or practiced, timing when known, setting, events, triggers, what helped, and what worsened the state. Then consider emotional, relational, spiritual, or practical meaning. Last, choose small behavioral changes. Distinguish lingering anxiety, sleep disruption, derealization/depersonalization, visual symptoms, shame/social disconnection, existential shock, trauma material, psychosis-like beliefs, and somatic hyperarousal rather than collapsing them into one story. Seek appropriate professional or medical support when symptoms are severe, persistent, or dangerous.
