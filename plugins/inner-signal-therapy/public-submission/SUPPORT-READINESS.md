Status: **externally blocked**.

## Prepared

- The package identity and current skills-only capability boundary are documented.
- Synthetic reviewer prompts need no credentials, private network, or hidden context.
- The readiness packet distinguishes static advisory behavior from unavailable storage or

## Required before a public submission

- Owner selects and staffs a support channel.
- A public HTTPS support URL is published under the same identity used for submission.
- Support copy explains how to report package defects and safety concerns without sending
  private therapy conversations.
- Escalation and response expectations are reviewed for the intended availability regions.
- The final support URL is inserted into the OpenAI listing and public policy materials.

No support URL, contact address, response-time promise, or publisher identity is invented in
this preparation packet.
