# Contributing

Public contributions are welcome through focused task branches and pull requests. Accepted contributions are licensed under the repository's MIT License.

Before changing files, read `AGENTS.md`, `.github/codex-repository.json`, `state/CODEX-CURRENT-STATE.md`, and the applicable nested instructions. Use the exact Node/npm toolchain and bootstrap command declared in the profile.

Every pull request must identify acceptance criteria, non-goals, focused and complete verification, risk and rollback, privacy and `stable` effects, current-state updates, residual uncertainty, and lesson disposition. Functional behavior begins with a failing regression. Provider-backed checks remain explicit and never receive credentials through ordinary CI.

Contribution does not grant authority over owner-gated therapy/framework policy, owner decision cards, model roles, privacy scope, or stable release approval. Infrastructure and contribution review must not silently cross those boundaries.
