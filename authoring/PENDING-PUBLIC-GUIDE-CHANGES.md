## Pending bundle A — experience, checking, social authenticity, and adverse-state lessons

Upstream authority: `guides/inner-child-guide-2026-10-04-r4.txt`, `AMEND.CROSS.EXPERIENCE_INTERPRETATION_CHOICE`, `AMEND.CROSS.RELATIONAL_PRACTICE_AUTHENTICITY`, `AMEND.CROSS.PROTECTIVE_ALARM_CALIBRATION`, `AMEND.CROSS.PSYCHOACTIVE_ADVERSE_TRACK_RECORD`, and `AMEND.CROSS.OUTCOME_HORIZON_ATTRIBUTION`.

### PGQ-001 — Experience is not its own interpretation
Preserve the distinction between a thought/sensation/attraction/aversion being real as an experience and the conclusion drawn from it. Separate experience, interpretation, and chosen action. Do not let the therapist, body, or strongest feeling of the hour decide which transient state is the person's "real" identity. Do not steer sexuality, orientation, or gender identity in either direction, and do not dismiss genuine identity-relevant experience merely because it is confusing, unwanted, or stigmatized.

### PGQ-002 — Social practice should be reciprocal, not a performance test
Preserve the warning that dates, approaches, confessions, sexual encounters, and other "exposures" can function as behavioral reassurance when their hidden job is to prove normality, attractiveness, lovability, orientation, safety, or intimacy capacity. Useful exposure and ordinary contact can continue while the certainty-checking function is dropped.

### PGQ-003 — Honesty is graded by earned trust
Preserve: privacy is not fakery; total disclosure is not required. Let people earn deeper access through demonstrated handling of smaller truths, boundaries, and vulnerability. When relationship depth requires maintaining a false normal/happy/perfect persona, either increase truthful contact at a safe pace or reduce depth. Acceptance/nonjudgment can be powerful without proving love, compatibility, or trust.

### PGQ-004 — Anxiety can protect without becoming an oracle
Preserve the distinction between protective intent and predictive accuracy. Anxiety may warn about overload, humiliation, uncertainty, danger, or a boundary, but it does not automatically establish the feared conclusion. Listening is not the same as obeying, and testing a prediction never overrides consent or a protector's no to touch, sex, deeper processing, or altered-state work.

### PGQ-005 — Match outcome claims to duration
Preserve the concrete reader rule: brief relief followed by recurrence is evidence of brief relief, not resolution or proof of mechanism. Keep any outcome tracking bounded enough that tracking itself does not become a checking ritual.

### PGQ-006 — Psychoactive adverse history changes planning
Preserve the proportional rule already in current authority: one prior psychotic-type reaction or persisting perceptual reaction is enough to gate retesting that same substance pending professional assessment and should inform consideration of other psychoactives capable of disturbing reality testing. Repeated episodes are not required; this is not a blanket anti-drug rule and does not authorize abrupt discontinuation of prescribed/dependence-producing substances.

### PGQ-007 — A healthy mind is not a perfectly clean mind
Preserve the current guide's mental-perfectionism correction: unwanted, contradictory, shameful, or unskillful thoughts/sensations can occur without being endorsement or identity. Mature guidance notices what appears, chooses skillful/value-consistent action, and lets the rest pass without turning occurrence into a permanent verdict. Preserve the current Buddhist framing around intention, training, repair, and discernment rather than mental purity.

Suggested reader-facing material to merge with that section without duplicating its existing strategy-review language:

> There is another kind of mismatch to watch for: the inner-child exercise may actually go very well while ordinary life stays almost unchanged. ## Pending bundle D — blocked action, conversational continuity, and memory support

Upstream authority: `AMEND.CROSS.BLOCKED_ACTION_WAIT`, `AMEND.CROSS.SUPPORT_MODE_CONTINUITY`, `AMEND.CROSS.MEMORY_VS_REFLECTIVE_JOURNALING`, `AMEND.CROSS.PRACTICAL_FOCUS_SEQUENCING`, and the corresponding runtime/graph rules.

### PGQ-014 — Some real problems are blocked, not actionable yet
Preserve the reader-facing distinction between a concrete problem that has a feasible next action and one whose currently relevant steps are all blocked by a known external prerequisite, permission, timing condition, or unavailable resource. In the blocked state, preserve the actual constraint chain and the condition that would reopen action rather than inventing a workaround that assumes a known constraint away. Waiting for a real external condition is not failed motivation or avoidance; put available effort into another useful priority until the route reopens.

### PGQ-015 — Remember a stated support mode and avoid stale advice
Preserve the conversational principle that an explicit preference for listening, collaborative untangling, advice/ideas, or a mix should be remembered until changed rather than repeatedly re-asked. Do not infer this preference from gender or another stereotype. When substantially the same guidance was already given and new information does not alter its rationale or feasibility, reference it briefly and move forward instead of presenting the old advice as a new intervention.

### PGQ-016 — Memory support is not the same as reflective journaling
Preserve the distinction between meaning-making journals and low-interpretation external memory. When reflective journaling makes experience more confusing, a short dated fact log, what-helped note, reminder, next-step entry, or voice note can preserve continuity without requiring simultaneous interpretation. Pattern-finding can happen later if useful; a memory aid does not need to explain the person's life.

### PGQ-017 — One blocked problem should not block the whole life
Preserve the reader-facing distinction between separate practical lanes. When several real problems coexist, one can be externally blocked while another still has a useful next step. Choose the current practical focus from the person's stated goal, impact, urgency, leverage, and what can actually move now; keep the blocked lane's reopening condition visible without letting it suppress an independent actionable priority. When overwhelm makes everything feel scrambled, reduce the sorting burden: one short organizing statement, one prioritized next step, and only a clarification whose answer would genuinely change that next step. Do not make the person define ordinary words or listen to a long recap when the practical implication is already clear enough.

## Pending bundle I — reparenting is the relationship, not the exercise

### PGQ-037 — Reparenting is not an exercise
Caused by: the owner's correction of 16 Oct 2026 (issue #168: “Reparenting is not an exercise”), `AMEND.IC.REPARENTING_NOT_AN_EXERCISE_20261016`, the retitled map overlay `OVERLAY.IC.PROTOCOL_STATE_PROVENANCE` and the served rule section “Reparenting and exercises”. A supervisor-facing assistant had described reparenting as an optional add-on to feeling safe and expressing oneself, and had treated an imagery meditation as the reparenting.
Teaching point: Reparenting isn't an exercise you do or finish; it's the ongoing relationship in which the adult you are now cares for, validates, protects and guides the younger, vulnerable part of you, through truthful boundaries, repair and everyday choices as much as through any meditation. Learning to feel safe inside while you say what you really feel, need and won't accept is that work, not a separate goal it might help with. Meditations, imagery, inner dialogue and affirmations are ways of practicing it, not its definition, its entry ticket or its scorecard: a session with no tears or relief doesn't mean nothing is happening, and the right words in a session don't by themselves mean it's done.
