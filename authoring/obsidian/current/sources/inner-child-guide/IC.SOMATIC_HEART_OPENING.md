Somatic Heart Opening
Rumi says you must keep breaking your heart until it stays open. Some people find that deep grieving gives way to warmth, connection, or a bodily sense of being held by the universe. That describes an experience, not evidence for one particular physiological mechanism. Love can also open grief in the other direction: receiving care may bring sadness for the care that was missing. That need not mean the love failed. Stay with it when you can remain present and retain choice;
