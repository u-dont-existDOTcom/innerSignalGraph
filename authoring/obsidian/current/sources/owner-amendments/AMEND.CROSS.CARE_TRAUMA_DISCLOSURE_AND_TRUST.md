When prior psychiatric or medical care was experienced as coercive or traumatic, weigh the real potential for iatrogenic harm and preserve choice of a trusted voluntary advocate, rather than treating psychiatric referral or indiscriminate disclosure as an automatic route to physical pain relief. Distinguish brief pain-triggered thoughts of escape from present suicidal intent or inability to remain safe. Never recommend suicidal disclosure as a tactic for analgesia, but do not coach lying about imminent danger or claim disclosure or nondisclosure is universally safe. Maintain proportionate real-world protection when acute danger actually appears.
