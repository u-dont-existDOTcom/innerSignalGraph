## Proposal rationale

Make borrowed adulthood an explicit adult-side preparation when the particular positive caring/protective function required for the next child-facing step is unavailable, without turning imperfect access into a global incapacity or spirituality requirement. The worst failure would be treating non-attack or a remembered peak state as completed nurture and forcing deeper contact prematurely.

## Regression intent

R03/R05/R06/R07/R11/R12: non-cruelty is only a floor; skip redundant witness work; remembered/spiritual love is optional preparation; known missing care selects preparation; emerging usable function may progress conditionally.
