# intuition-calibration-r1

## Intent

Make two owner-approved distinctions explicit in executable routing. First, suicidal desire or intent, once immediate danger and fusion are sufficiently contained for reflection, must receive the serious Buddhist reality check that human birth is an extraordinarily rare and precious opportunity; this is not gated by prior spiritual curiosity and is not reduced to merely saying life might be worthwhile. Second, external-guide and intuition decisions must integrate intuitive/gestalt/affective cognition with analytical/contradiction-sensitive checking so praise, mirroring, pity, confession, charisma, rescue hopes, attraction, humor, or mystical ease cannot silently convert adverse evidence into trust.

## Non-goals

Do not teach indiscriminate distrust of intuition, spirituality, teachers, humor, vulnerability, compassion, mystical cognition, or communities. Do not make analytical cognition the sole authority or literalize the distinction as simplistic left-brain/right-brain anatomy. Do not diagnose an outside person as manipulative from one influence cue. Do not invent postmortem odds, guarantee hell, suppress urgent real-world suicide safety, or turn Buddhist teachings into a forced conversion requirement.

## Worst plausible failure

The trust-calibration branch could become paranoid hypervigilance that treats warmth, confession, joking, intuition, or spiritual gifts as evidence of manipulation, or could become a new analytical compulsion that disables useful intuitive cognition. The suicide branch could become shame-based spiritual coercion or falsely certain claims about an individual afterlife outcome. The semantic-asset pipeline could also let owner descriptions silently invent visual claims that are not actually present.

## Acceptance distinctions

A safe, sober suicidal person receives self/death inquiry and the precious-human-opportunity reality check even when spiritual curiosity is absent; an unsafe, disoriented, unable-to-stop, or actively altered person remains in immediate safety/orientation first. Strong intuition that is aligned with a positive track record is not pathologized. Strong intuition that contradicts the user's own adverse evidence, or external authority pull that makes contradiction stop mattering, activates calibration. Admission of dishonesty or danger can count as self-awareness without erasing the current risk. Humor and mystical cognition remain allowed, but consequential factual claims, requests, permissions, and trust transfers remain answerable to evidence and correction. Analytical dismissal of meaningful intuitive information can also activate calibration, proving that neither cognitive mode receives unilateral authority.
