# reparenting-strategy-refinement-20260925

## Intent

Preserve the reparenting target while replacing automatic count-driven abandonment with evidence-bound diagnosis and supported refinement. Make borrowed adulthood an executable preparation sequence when the positive caring/protective function required for deeper child-facing work is currently unavailable. Keep non-cruelty as a floor rather than proof of nurture, distinguish remembered love from present usable care and child reception, and resume the parent task once the missing function is actually usable instead of restarting the whole bootstrap.

## Non-goals

Do not weaken immediate safety, child-contact permission, consent, withdrawal, ordinary external-action precedence, memory-source caution, provider/delivery assessment, or scoped falsification. Do not require spirituality, prayer, altered states, warm love, a perfect inner adult, or outside-helper authority. Do not convert unclear progress into proof that a method works, and do not preserve a method indefinitely through post-hoc prerequisites or unfalsifiable explanations.

## Worst plausible failure

The repair could become a method-preservation bias: every disappointing result might be relabeled as a missing prerequisite or refinement, blocking legitimate replacement. The inverse failure is also material: generic deep-work readiness, an accepted task, non-retaliation, or remembered peak love could be mistaken for the positive caring/protective capacity actually needed for a child-facing step, causing premature or coercive deeper work.

## Acceptance distinctions

Mixed, flat, delayed, or low-information progress without a source-bound defect remains diagnostic rather than an automatic switch. A concrete implementation gap may refine the current method while retaining its target and history. Adequately exposed contradicted predictions, refusal, meaningful harm, infeasibility, or a supported better route retain scoped switching force. When a needed caring/protective function is absent, borrowed adulthood runs adult-side first; when enough current function is already usable, preparation is not restarted. Remembered or spiritual love may be an optional sober resource but is not present-access proof and never authorizes substance use, metaphysical certainty, or forced child reception.
