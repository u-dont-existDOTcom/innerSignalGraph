## Proposal rationale

Separate ordinary/profound love, remembered access, present access, and child reception. The worst failure would be turning a past spiritual or substance-associated opening into a compulsory route or a claim of current readiness.

## Regression intent

R06/R08/R09/R10/P09: past profound love is a sober optional resource; secular/goodwill routes remain valid; refusal is not forced; no substance recommendation.
