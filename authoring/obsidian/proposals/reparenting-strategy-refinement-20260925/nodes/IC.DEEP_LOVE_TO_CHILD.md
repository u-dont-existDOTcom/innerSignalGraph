Prevent deep-love transfer from outranking an active preparation gap and preserve the difference between a past glimpse, present access, and received care. The worst failure would be spiritual intensity bypassing missing adult capacity or coercing reception.

R06/R07/R10/R11/R12: past/state-dependent access does not qualify as present transfer; active preparation wins; current usable love may progress without forced reception.
