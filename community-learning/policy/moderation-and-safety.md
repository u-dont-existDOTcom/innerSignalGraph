# InnerSignal Commons moderation and safety contract

**Status:** Pilot implementation contract. Human moderation procedures and jurisdiction-specific emergency resources must be completed before a network-accessible pilot.

## Community norm

> Speak from experience. Say what happened, under what conditions, and what you are uncertain about. Do not diagnose another member or tell them what they must do.

## Response contracts

Authors select one response contract per post. The server restricts reply types to that contract rather than relying only on etiquette.

- `listen-only`: witness/support replies only.
- `similar-experiences`: witness and similar-experience replies.
- `questions-welcome`: witness, similar experiences, and questions.
- `practical-ideas`: the preceding categories plus practical ideas.
- `challenge-interpretation`: all bounded reply categories, including challenge.
- `help-field-note`: witness, questions, and practical help structuring a Field Note.

## Deterministic containment

The pilot performs narrow pattern checks for:

- possible imminent self-harm or harm to others;
- dangerous medication or dosing instructions;
- coercive hypnosis or influence without consent;
- claims that hypnosis or regression proved a recovered memory;
- email addresses or phone numbers;
- commercial or payment solicitation.

A match does not establish that the content is unsafe or false. It changes publication status to `held-for-human-review`. The author can still see the item; other participants cannot.

## Human review requirements before network launch

A moderator console must support:

- publish, edit-with-author, retain-held, or remove decisions;
- documented reasons and appeals;
- urgent escalation without declaring causality;
- country-aware crisis-resource presentation;
- third-party privacy review;
- predatory practitioner and commercial promotion review;
- moderator rotation, maximum exposure limits, debriefing, and supervision;
- incident retention and deletion rules.

AI triage may prioritize a queue but must not be the sole crisis, safety, diagnosis, or recovered-memory decision maker.

## Prohibited product paths

Community content must not be introduced into an active therapy formulation, hypnotic induction, inward route, waking return, or private journal without a deliberate user action. Peer stories cannot be used to infer another participant's diagnosis, childhood events, abuse, memories, or internal roles.
