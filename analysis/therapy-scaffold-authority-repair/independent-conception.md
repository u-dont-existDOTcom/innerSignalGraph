# Independent conception snapshot

Recorded before implementation or broader-case selection on 2026-08-24.

## Problem

The current runtime turns a rich human interaction into an audited categorical snapshot and authoritative deterministic plan, then tells the final renderer that reasoning is already complete. This may suppress relational insight and force unnecessary procedural coverage.

## Demonstrated minimum

Keeping the existing upstream scaffold while making the final renderer independently reconsider the case improved blinded preference on A001.

## Promising extension

Formulating from raw language before graph audit reconstructed higher-order relational insight more reliably, but was not directly compared with the simpler realization-only repair.

## Decision principle

Prefer the simplest architecture that produces a robust quality improvement without weakening hard safety, epistemic constraints, routing correctness, continuity, latency, or recoverability.

## Information-preservation hypothesis

The observed A001 trace first weakened at audited extraction. That does not prove audit compression is the sole cause. The production-candidate comparison must retain raw semantic formulation, raw categorical extraction, audit deltas, corrected variables, plan, graph audit, and final integration trace separately so the first weakening or recovery point can be identified without silently overwriting upstream evidence.

## Evidence boundary

The prior ablation is a single-case stochastic engineering experiment, not clinical validation. Its result justifies the broader C-versus-D comparison; it does not authorize production activation, new therapy policy, a graph rewrite, or a claim of therapeutic outcome.
