# Independent conception snapshot

Recorded before changing the experiment harness or consulting therapy literature for this task. This is a diagnostic hypothesis, not an approved therapy policy or clinical claim.

## Problem

A strong therapy map can contain the right concepts while the runtime still produces mediocre therapy because formulation has been compressed into an intermediate representation before the strongest interpretive reasoning occurs.

## Candidate mechanism

```text
human experience
→ model extraction into enumerated variables
→ deterministic graph matching
→ selected primary/secondary jobs
→ mandatory realization
→ prose
```

Potential information loss occurs at extraction/planning and is then locked in because the final renderer is told not to reformulate.

## Competing explanation

The scaffold is fine and the quality difference is mostly model capability (Sonnet/Codex versus GPT-5.6 Sol/Opus).
