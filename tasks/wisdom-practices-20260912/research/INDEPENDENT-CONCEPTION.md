# InnerSignal wisdom practices — independent conception snapshot
Captured 2026-09-12 before the new literature pass. Design research only; not installed therapy policy.

The owner's proposed practice can alter understanding, felt identity/state, and the sense of being accompanied by a wiser self. These are all useful entrances, not mutually exclusive categories.

Owner's new sequence: mentally write what one wants to say now; save that private draft; take an editor's position; compose what should actually be said now; check it before sending; then return to the original draft for inner-child reparenting. The return to the original feeling is integral, not disposal of an unacceptable self.

Other owner-reported practices: append “And so that's the way it happened!” to a painful autobiographical memory, possibly preceded by “Once upon a time…”, to experience narrative distance; recall shared human suffering and universal human longings; look for a genuinely useful kernel in criticism without treating the surrounding insult as truth; recall a past instance of responding well to a greater difficulty as a source of present capacity.

Open questions: transfer from temporary distance to compassionate action; editor versus internal censor; helpful narrative closure versus self-dismissal or dissociation; universality versus minimization; evidence for the exact short phrases rather than adjacent methods; past competence without pressure to reproduce it under different conditions.
