# Human usefulness and harm evaluation — not yet established

The deterministic candidate can prove schema validity, process/channel traceability, counter retention, routing precedence, source inclusion, and rejection of a small set of explicit overclaims. It cannot prove that a person feels understood, that a channel helps, that a metaphor remains genuinely user-owned, that a plain-language bridge preserves contact, or that subtle symbolic inflation and coercion are absent.

A future separately authorized evaluation should include diverse human review of CLEAR, EXPERIENTIAL, and BRIDGE responses; multilingual and indirect overclaim cases; consent and decline handling; dissociation/mania/psychosis-sensitive stabilization; ordinary-life transfer; and delayed adverse effects. Reviewers must score helpfulness and harm separately from structural adherence. Vividness, emotional intensity, preference, and short-term relief must not substitute for client-generated information, agency, functioning, or durable movement.

No human-client, clinician, facilitator, or live-model evaluation was performed in this Iteration-lane task.
