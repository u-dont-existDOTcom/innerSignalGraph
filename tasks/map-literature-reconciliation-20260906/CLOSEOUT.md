## What is now implemented

- Current-issue relational completion and reopening; distinguish a live emotional/practical issue from redundant checking around it. A relationship topic or unfinished grief no longer automatically forbids disengagement from a separate useless loop.
- A protective response can remain present while permitting an agreed small step. Presence, permission, readiness and consent are not collapsed together.
- A current task carries phase, agreement, evidence references, the last reported response, and relevant action/emotion details. Corrections, changed issue scope and withdrawn evidence invalidate obsolete state. No separate memory backend was added.
- Canonical questions respect immediate safety, known answers and explicit absence. A no-question contract no longer falls through to stale adjudication. Unknown capacity alone no longer selects deficit/preparation exercises.
- Required interventions are distinct from contextual diagram nodes. Bounded task guidance and success/change-point signals now reach the responder instead of relying on source-reference labels alone.
- Existing action, Protector and apprentice routes handle feasible planning, actual attempts, barriers, consequences and adjustment. Existing emotional work handles care mismatch, valid correction/anger/grief, partial shifts, delight and exploration. Emotion-focused work is not confused with tapping.
- One new `IC.SPIRITUAL_STRUGGLE` job addresses distress or loss within a valued sacred relationship. Borrowed spiritual love, mature devotion and relinquishing inappropriate control are preserved.

## Test the source fidelity, not just obedience to the map

The runnable supplement is `tasks/guide-fidelity-20260906/`. Twelve original development scenarios contain 23 user turns. Three isolated conditions use the same explicitly pinned responder: the normal candidate pipeline; full authoritative guide/amendments without the graph; and the normal pipeline with full source supplied at realization. The reference condition is not an oracle. Separate stochastic histories/extractions mean arm differences are diagnostic, not proof that source delivery alone caused a change; use saved-plan replay to isolate a disputed context effect.

Two independently called grader identities, distinct from the responder and each other, assess actual useful behavior against the relevant source passages. They do not see the arm, model or expected graph node. Exact source/answer references are recorded. The graders must first reject four deliberately wrong controls and accept two reasonable ones. Blockers are not averaged away; disagreements and source conflicts remain visible. A model claiming a node was realized is not sufficient evidence. Calibration fixtures are not bot outputs, and source/quote checks alone do not establish semantic truth.

The trace retains case extraction, task/route selection, delivered instructions, raw and enforced answers, source/config/runtime hashes and call usage. This supports separating source gaps, extraction loss, routing errors, missing task detail, poor realization and enforcement corruption. Cases are development cases seen during implementation, not a held-out or clinical trial.
