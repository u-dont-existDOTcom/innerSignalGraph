# Owner adoption: current inner-child guide research edits

Recorded 2026-09-07 on draft PR #46.

## Decision

The owner explicitly adopted **all twelve** editorial operations E01-E12 in `GUIDE-EDITORIAL-PATCH-2026-09-06.md` for the current inner-child guide pasted in the owner conversation. Treat those operations as **OWNER-APPROVED ARTICLE WORDING**, not as suggestions awaiting another approval round.

Adopted operations:

1. **E01 — Helper disagreement:** disagreement/correction is not automatically resistance; a helper must be able to reconsider its interpretation or method without using disagreement as self-confirming evidence.
2. **E02 — When More Processing Becomes the Hook:** distinguish live emotional work from repetitive checking/rumination around it; therapy practices must not become compulsory rituals before returning to life.
3. **E03 — Responsive care:** evaluate how care is experienced, not only the words; change distance, touch, voice, source or method when care feels intrusive rather than treating mismatch as proof of inability to receive love.
4. **E04 — Spiritual struggle and mature relinquishment:** prayer/spiritual relationship can itself be where pain occurs; do not automatically call this bypass, weak faith or parental projection; distinguish responsibility from outcomes/control that can appropriately be relinquished.
5. **E05 — Delight as Nurturer function:** the child should eventually be enjoyed and delighted in, not only comforted in distress or managed when difficult.
6. **E06 — Action planning and review:** make a chosen Protector action concrete enough to try, then review actual barriers and consequences; noncompletion is not automatically resistance/weak adulthood and completion is not automatically evidence of benefit.
7. **E07 — Follow the change point:** a protector can remain present while allowing a bounded next step; follow what has newly become available instead of restarting the original intervention; partial change counts.
8. **E08 — Overcontrol is not developed adulthood:** forced discipline without needs, joy or closeness can be another developmental problem; sometimes the adult act is flexibility, receiving help, expressing a preference or tolerating safe imperfection. Preserve accountability and reciprocity.
9. **E09 — Somatic Heart Opening evidence correction:** retain the reported experience of grief giving way to warmth/connection while removing the unsupported specific ventral-vagal/cardiac-plexus biological-rebound explanation; care can itself evoke grief and tears alone do not prove deeper healing.
10. **E10 — EFT terminology:** use `tapping (Emotional Freedom Techniques)` where the guide means tapping, so it cannot be confused with emotion-focused therapy.
11. **E11 — Forgiveness timing:** return to causal inquiry over time without forcing it or endlessly rehearsing the same case.
12. **E12 — Related Clinical Work:** add the concise appendix note distinguishing original framework/contributors from later comparison/adaptation across schema therapy, CFT, attachment repair, MI, mentalizing, MCT, BA, RO DBT, emotion-focused therapy and spiritually integrated psychotherapy.

The exact copy and placement anchors are authoritative in `GUIDE-EDITORIAL-PATCH-2026-09-06.md`. Do not paraphrase the article edits when the exact adopted wording is available there.
