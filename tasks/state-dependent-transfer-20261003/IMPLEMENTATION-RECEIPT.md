# State-dependent altered-state transfer implementation receipt

Status: implementation candidate on a task branch; not installed or released.

## Owner outcome

Add explicit preparation, in-state handling, sober transfer, and repetition-motive routing for therapeutic capacities or meanings that become experientially credible in altered states but fade or become inaccessible afterward.

## Implemented contract

- Track sober transfer as state_bound, fading, partial, integrated, or not in play.
- Track repetition motive as genuinely new exploration, restore-access, mixed, or not in play.
- Prepare relevant Nurturer / Protector / Guide / guard / closure capacity before planned altered-state work without demanding a particular revelation.
- During coherent, physically stable altered-state therapy, preserve newly accessible private therapeutic meaning without repeatedly interrupting it with forensic caveats.
- Keep consequential external claims evidence-bound and subject to sober review.
- Afterward, target usable sober carryover rather than reproduction of the altered state itself.
- Before another altered-state session, distinguish new work from primarily regaining a known state, conviction, love, memory access, or relationship.
- Do not infer addiction, avoidance, dependence, or failed integration from repetition alone.
- Do not claim sober transfer replaces altered-state therapy for everyone or has been shown to reduce session count.
