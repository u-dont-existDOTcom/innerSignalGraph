# Threat-pathway and risk-calibrated engagement prior-work scan

Status: independent conception preserved; bounded external scan complete.

## Independent conception — recorded before the external scan

The missing product mechanism is not a keyword-to-crisis classifier. It is one evidence-bound, revisable controller that separates violent thought or grievance from movement toward action, preserves the information channel and direct therapeutic engagement when no operational indicators are established, and increases intervention only as behavior supports increasing risk. Its state must survive context compaction without becoming a permanent dangerousness label.

The controller should compose the existing structured extraction, adversarial audit, durable longitudinal state, Path Performance Controller, graph safety precedence, and realization contract. It should not create a separate clinical diagnosis, universal legal-reporting rule, or violence-specific therapy graph. The strongest falsifiers are the inverse failures: generic emergency escalation from ideation alone, and continued abstract engagement after concrete target research, means acquisition, date selection, or stated intent.

Political/systemic material needs a related but distinct causal check: a named public figure can act as a symbolic compression of distributed incentives, institutions, beliefs, and coordination failures. The response should neither deny systemic harm nor let a single-enemy story erase personal, relational, community, institutional, or political leverage. Identity capture is about the regulatory function and deterioration of the rest of life, not politics, religion, sports, or ideology as categories.

## Bounded external scan

The scan was deliberately limited to official threat-assessment guidance and peer-reviewed clinical or structured-professional-judgment literature. It was used after the independent design above was recorded.

- The FBI's [Making Prevention a Reality](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/making_prevention_a_reality_identifying_assessing_managing_threats_of_ta.pdf) describes a pathway from grievance through ideation, research/planning, preparation, breach, and attack. It also warns that no single behavior is dispositive and treats concern as dynamic, with mitigating and protective factors relevant to management.
- The FBI's [Pathway to Intended Violence Quick Reference Guide](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/piv-qrg.pdf) makes two limits especially important for this implementation: stages need not be linear, and `not observed` must not be confused with `absent`. InnerSignal therefore records `unknown` separately from explicit denial and never invents a positive indicator.
- The FBI's [2025 U.S. Violent Extremism Mobilization Indicators](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/counterterrorism/us-violent-extremism-mobilization-indicators-2025-pdf.pdf) distinguishes observable preparation or mobilization from advocacy, activism, strong rhetoric, and general philosophical embrace. The associated [critical-infrastructure supplement](https://www.fbi.gov/file-repository/counterterrorism/us-violent-extremism-mobilization-indicators-critical-infrastructure-sector.pdf) supplies concrete behavioral examples such as target research or surveillance, weapon acquisition, concealment, and rehearsal.
- The U.S. Secret Service NTAC [Behavioral Threat Assessment Units guide](https://www.secretservice.gov/sites/default/files/reports/2024-10/Behavioral-Threat-Assessment-Units-A-Guide-for-State-and-Local-Law-Enforcement-to-Prevent-Targeted-Violence.pdf) is consistent with behavior- and context-based assessment. Its law-enforcement setting limits direct transfer to a therapeutic app.
- The APA's [Gun Violence: Prediction, Prevention, and Policy](https://www.apa.org/pubs/reports/gun-violence-prevention) supports asking whether a person is planning, preparing, or moving toward an attack rather than profiling them, and emphasizes the false-positive problem in predicting rare events. Its [threat-assessment overview](https://www.apa.org/monitor/2014/02/cover-threat) similarly frames the work as contextual prevention rather than categorical prediction.
- Wortzel and colleagues' peer-reviewed [Therapeutic Risk Management of the Violent Patient](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33275387/) supports temporally and severity-stratified formulation, examining the function and chain of violent ideation or behavior, and individualized safety planning. A related [clinical assessment article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32692128/) supports combining interview evidence with structured methods rather than treating a phrase as a diagnosis.
- Structured-professional-judgment literature on [dynamic risk and protective factors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3727954/) supports retaining current mitigators and protective factors rather than a permanent dangerousness label.
- Adjacent suicide-disclosure research reports that perfunctory or templated assessment can inhibit disclosure while empathy, a clear rationale, and straightforward language can support it ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23580131/)). This is used only as a rapport-design caution, not as direct validation for homicidal-ideation handling.

## Existing-work map and disposition

Disposition: **COMPOSE / ADAPT**.

- COMPOSE the owner's three required distinctions with the repository's existing evidence-bound extraction, adversarial audit, Path Performance monitoring, safety precedence, durable state, and realization fidelity checks.
- ADAPT the external pathway and dynamic-risk concepts into a non-diagnostic engineering controller with explicit `unknown`, direct-observation provenance, current mitigators, bounded reassessment, and proportionate routing.
- RETAIN the owner's systems/agency and rapport principles as constitutional steering requirements. These are broader than the external threat-assessment sources and are not presented as findings from them.
- REJECT a numeric violence-prediction score, keyword escalation, profiling, a permanent dangerousness label, and any universal reporting rule. The cited sources do not justify those product behaviors.

## Claims boundary

This scan can inform a candidate engineering policy. It cannot clinically validate the app, establish a universal prediction rule, turn law-enforcement guidance into therapy, or create jurisdiction-independent reporting duties. The controller is a steering and evidence-discipline mechanism, not a diagnosis or actuarial instrument.
