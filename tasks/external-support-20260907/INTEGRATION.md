The current owner request makes meaningful contribution a core support function when capacity permits, separates ordinary work exchange from supported care, and requires reversible environmental-fit trials. Peer relationships require assessment of reciprocity and rescue burden without categorical abandonment. An interactive compatibility interview and prior-treatment failure model precede profile, explicit owner policy, archetype selection, discovery, verification, fit ranking, action and follow-up. Evidence and owner/user values stay distinct.

Predictions: a high-need synthetic person must not receive an ordinary farm stay as care; lower-need stable people can consider vetted exchanges; unknown critical facts cannot become approval; environmental failure revises fit without blaming the person; unaffordable offers cannot erase benefit from a low-cost method; no fitting program produces gaps and modular support, never constraint relaxation. Trial durations are owner-proposed reversible defaults, not validated treatment doses. Remaining uncertainties include actual resource availability, human assessment, affordable supervision, recognition of adverse effects and real-world outcomes.
