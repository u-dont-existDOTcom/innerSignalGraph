## Implemented framework changes

- Experience, interpretation, and chosen action are distinct. Intrusive or contradictory mental content is not automatically identity, endorsement, love, danger, morality, or instruction.
- Repeat certainty requests do not receive endless reassurance; checking can occur through behavior as well as thought.
- Useful exposure/ERP remains available: remove checking rather than escalating a test or avoiding the situation. Genuine sexuality/orientation/gender/relationship exploration is not classified as checking merely because it is uncertain.
- Social practice remains reciprocal. Privacy is not fakery; disclosure depth follows demonstrated trust and safety. Healthy support and co-regulation remain available.
- Protective anxiety is informative but not an oracle. Ordinary-life predictions may be tested, but consent, immediate safety, possible medical red flags, and a protector's no to deeper/altered work retain precedence.
- The Guide does not demand mental purity. Skillful chosen action, learning, repair, and future restraint are kept separate from permanent identity verdicts.
- Severe psychoactive adverse history changes planning: one psychotic-type/persisting perceptual reaction gates retest pending professional assessment and informs other reality-disturbing psychoactives without becoming a blanket anti-drug rule. Abrupt stopping of prescribed medication or a physically depended-on substance is not advised.
- Benefit and causal claims are matched to duration/carryover. Brief relief is not resolution or mechanism proof; tracking remains brief so it does not become checking.
