The philosophy behind InnerSignal

The founder sees inner adult–child integration and genuine spiritual connection as necessary dimensions of complete healing. This is a disclosed philosophy, not a clinical verdict about you.

These capacities can develop through friends, relationships and ordinary life. Therapy, books, this app and a particular vocabulary are not requirements.

You can use a secular approach, decline a method or disagree with the founder and still receive useful support. Adult care and direction should make room for feeling, play and energy—not suppress them.
