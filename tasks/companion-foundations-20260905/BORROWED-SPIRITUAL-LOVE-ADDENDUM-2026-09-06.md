# Borrowed spiritual love: implementation addendum

Recorded 2026-09-06 from explicit owner approval. This refines the existing `Influence / Protection / Love` route; it does not create a new modality department, change stable, merge, deploy, or establish metaphysical/clinical truth.

## Why this is an extension, not a new route

The current cross-guide graph already contains `ROUTE.INFLUENCE_LOVE_CAPACITY`. It activates when an experience is reported as other-than-self and direct mettā is limited or inaccessible. Its existing recommendations already permit borrowing a bounded function from a spiritual figure and then returning authority/capacity to the person.

The owner clarification specifies what that borrowing means and what it does **not** mean.

## Approved developmental sequence

When direct love is inaccessible under fear, but a spiritually meaningful source of loving support is available, InnerSignal may help the user borrow love/protection through their own tradition or language—for example God, Jesus, angels, devas, saints, ancestors, or another loving presence.

Preferred sequence:

1. **Receive** — experience or imagine love/protection coming from the trusted spiritual source.
2. **Participate** — join the loving intention rather than remaining only a passive recipient; e.g. ask that the situation be filled with love rather than that an enemy be attacked.
3. **Generate** — practice producing some of the love directly, including toward the frightened inner child and, when appropriate within the spiritual practice, toward the feared presence.
4. **Internalize** — over time, carry more of the capacity personally so fear does not automatically make the person helpless without an external intervention.

This mirrors borrowed adulthood: receive -> observe/participate -> initiate -> internalize.

## Devotion is not dependency

Do **not** interpret ongoing prayer, devotion, surrender to God, reliance on grace, or continuing relationship with angels/devas/etc. as pathology or failed transfer. In many traditions, ongoing dependence on God is itself a mature spiritual ideal.

The relevant question is functional agency, not theological independence:

- Is the relationship helping the person become more able to access love, courage, discernment, and caring action?
- Or do they remain psychologically helpless unless an external spiritual rescuer is imagined to act for them?

The goal is not `I no longer need God/Jesus/devas/etc.` The goal is that spiritual relationship and personal capacity can coexist.

## Fallback when borrowed love is also inaccessible

If neither direct mettā nor borrowed spiritual love is genuinely accessible, do not escalate to psychic combat merely because the person is frightened. Route back to the smallest believable Nurturer/Protector/Guide function: care for the frightened child, restore orientation and agency, borrow ordinary adult support if useful, and build toward love without forcing a performance of fearlessness or affection.

## Ordinary-world protection remains independent

This route is only about the spiritual/nonordinary intervention logic. Prayer or borrowed spiritual love never replaces concrete protection in physical/social danger: leaving, locking a door, saying no, seeking human help, medical assessment, or other practical action remain independently available.

## Ontology remains open at the app level

InnerSignal may mirror the user's language—jinn, spirit, entity, presence, unattached burden, astral attack—without affirming or denying the underlying ontology. A helpful response to prayer or mettā does not prove what caused the experience.

## Routing implication

No new topological node is required. `ROUTE.INFLUENCE_LOVE_CAPACITY` remains the correct bounded route for direct mettā being limited/inaccessible. Future generated graph wording should preserve the sequence and devotion-vs-helplessness distinction above when that node is next regenerated or revised. Until then, this addendum and `CF-D014` are the controlling owner clarification for the task branch.
